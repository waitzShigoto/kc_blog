## 關鍵詞彙 (Key Vocabulary)

*本主題的重要詞彙與片語*

- **numerator**: 分子。分數中上方的數字，表示我們有多少部分。
  - 例句：In the fraction 3/4, the **numerator** is 3.（在分數 3/4 中，分子是 3。）

- **denominator**: 分母。分數中下方的數字，表示整體被分成多少等份。
  - 例句：The **denominator** tells us how many equal parts the whole is divided into.（分母告訴我們整體被分成多少等份。）

- **improper fraction**: 假分數。分子大於或等於分母的分數。
  - 例句：Seven fourths (7/4) is an **improper fraction**.（七分之四是一個假分數。）

- **proper fraction**: 真分數。分子小於分母的分數。
  - 例句：Three quarters (3/4) is a **proper fraction** because the numerator is less than the denominator.（四分之三是真分數，因為分子小於分母。）

- **mixed number**: 帶分數。由整數和真分數組成。
  - 例句：One and three quarters (1 3/4) is a **mixed number**.（一又四分之三是帶分數。）

## 實例與應用 (Examples & Application)

*提供實際例句或應用情境*

**例句一：烹飪場景**
> "The recipe calls for **three quarters** of a cup of sugar."  
> （食譜需要四分之三杯糖。）

**例句二：分享食物**
> "I ate **one third** of the pizza, and my sister ate **two thirds**."  
> （我吃了三分之一的披薩，我姐姐吃了三分之二。）

**例句三：數學課堂**
> "To simplify this fraction, we need to divide both the **numerator** and the **denominator** by their greatest common divisor."  
> （要簡化這個分數，我們需要將分子和分母都除以它們的最大公約數。）

**Student**: "A fraction represents a part of a whole. It has a **numerator** on top and a **denominator** on the bottom."

**Teacher**: "Excellent! Now, look at this fraction: 5/3. What kind of fraction is this?"  
**Student**: "This is an **improper fraction** because the **numerator** is greater than the **denominator**."

**發音注意事項：**
- "numerator" 的重音在第一音節：**NU**-mer-a-tor
- "denominator" 的重音在第三音節：de-nom-i-**NA**-tor
- 分數讀法：1/2 = "one half", 1/3 = "one third", 1/4 = "one quarter", 3/4 = "three quarters"

**常用表達：**
- 當分子是 1 時，分母用單數：one third, one fifth
- 當分子大於 1 時，分母用複數：two thirds, three fifths
- 特殊讀法：1/2 = "a half" 或 "one half", 1/4 = "a quarter" 或 "one quarter"
