As the 2026 World Baseball Classic™ (WBC) approaches, the most anticipated shopping destination for fans is about to arrive! Fanatics Japan has announced that starting **Friday, February 20, 2026**, limited-time "WORLD BASEBALL CLASSIC™ OFFICIAL STORE" locations will open at two Tokyo landmarks: **MIYASHITA PARK** in Shibuya and **TOKYO SKYTREE**.

Whether you are looking for Shohei Ohtani fan gear or want to collect high-quality merchandise from national teams around the world, this is a must-visit spot this spring!

---

## Official Store Information

The official stores will be open in Shibuya and Sumida Ward simultaneously. Please note that the exhibition periods differ slightly between the two locations:

### 1. WORLD BASEBALL CLASSIC™ OFFICIAL STORE -MIYASHITA PARK-
*   **Location:** MIYASHITA PARK 4F Multi-purpose Sports Facility (Sand Court) [1-26-5 Shibuya, Shibuya-ku, Tokyo]
*   **Period:** February 20 (Fri) – March 22 (Sun), 2026
*   **Hours:** 10:00 AM – 9:00 PM (Opens at 12:00 PM on the first day)
*   **Highlights:** The "World Baseball Classic™ Tokyo Fan Zone presented by dip" will also be held at the same venue.

### 2. WORLD BASEBALL CLASSIC™ OFFICIAL STORE -TOKYO SKYTREE-
*   **Location:** TOKYO SKYTREE 1F SKYTREE SPACE [1-1-2 Oshiage, Sumida-ku, Tokyo]
*   **Period:** February 20 (Fri) – March 18 (Wed), 2026
*   **Hours:** 10:00 AM – 9:00 PM (Special hours: 9:00 AM – 9:00 PM from Feb 21–23)

---

## Featured Products: Over 500 Choices

Designed with the atmosphere and excitement of a baseball stadium in mind, the store features over 500 high-quality official products, including:

*   **Samurai Japan Section:** Official name and number T-shirts and cheering towels for selected players such as **Shohei Ohtani**, **Yoshinobu Yamamoto**, and **Teruaki Sato**.
*   **Apparel and Accessories:** Limited edition jerseys, apparel collection, and caps featuring the WBC logo.
*   **Global Team Gear:** In addition to Japan, merchandise for team **USA** (last tournament's finalist) and other POOL C participants (Tokyo Pool presented by dip) like **Australia, South Korea, Czech Republic, and Chinese Taipei** will be available.
*   **Souvenirs and Goods:** Various snacks, sweets, and lifestyle goods perfect for gifts or personal use.

---

## Essential Tips: Lining Up and Payment Rules

To ensure a smooth shopping experience, please pay attention to the following regulations before visiting:

1.  **Strictly Cashless Payment (Cashless ONLY):** 
    **Cash is NOT accepted.** Please prepare the following payment methods:
    *   Credit Cards (IC chip cards only: Mastercard, Visa, American Express, JCB, etc. One-time payment only).
    *   Transportation IC Cards (Suica, Pasmo, etc.).
    *   QR Code Payments.

2.  **Lining Up Rules (No Overnight Lining Up):** 
    *   **MIYASHITA PARK:** The queue starts forming daily at 8:00 AM near the SOUTH 1F entrance.
    *   **TOKYO SKYTREE:** The queue starts forming 1 hour before opening near the Tower Yard 1F Group West Lobby entrance.
    *   **Follow staff instructions.** Numbered entry tickets may be issued if it becomes too crowded.

3.  **Purchase Limits and Stock:** 
    Popular items (such as Ohtani gear) may have purchase limits. Supplies are limited and available while stocks last. No reservations are accepted.
