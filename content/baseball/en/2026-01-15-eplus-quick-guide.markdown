## Quick Start: Must-Know Before Ticket Grabbing

### Ticket Purchase URLs

**Overseas (Recommended):**
- https://eplus.tickets/en/sf/ibt/detail/0260360001?P6=i00

**Domestic Japan:**
- https://eplus.jp/sf/word/0000101520

### Sale Time
- **Japan Time: January 15, 2026 19:00**
- **Taiwan Time: January 15, 2026 18:00**

### Key Advantage
e+ allows you to **complete all preparations before sale time**, and you only need to press the "Apply" button when sales start!

---

## Ticket Purchase Process Step by Step

### Step 1. Enter Purchase Page Before Sale (Recommend 30 Minutes Early)

**Operation Steps:**

1. Enter WBC ticket purchase page
2. Click **"Apply for tickets"**

![1.png](/images/wbc/1.png)

3. Find the desired match, click **"to the next"**

![2.png](/images/wbc/2.png)

4. Will enter seat description page

![3.png](/images/wbc/3.png)

---

### Step 2. Select Seats and Number of Tickets

Scroll down the page and start selecting:

**Date and time**
**Seat type**
**Number of tickets**

![4.png](/images/wbc/4.png)
![5.png](/images/wbc/5.png)
![6.png](/images/wbc/6.png)

**Important Recommendation: Select "Don't Choose Seats"**
- For popular performances, when choosing seats, tickets sell out very quickly
- Get something first, then optimize

Then click **"Apply"**

![7.png](/images/wbc/7.png)

Agreement terms will pop up, click **"Agree"**

![8.png](/images/wbc/8.png)

---

### Step 3. Enter Personal Information

**Need to fill in:**

1. **Personal Information**

![9.png](/images/wbc/9.png)

2. **Credit Card Information**

![10.png](/images/wbc/10.png)

3. **Residence, Passport Nationality**

![11.png](/images/wbc/11.png)

4. Finally click **"Go to setting"**

![12.png](/images/wbc/12.png)

---

### Step 4. Wait for Sale, Press "Apply" on Time

**Critical Moment!**

After completing the above steps:
- Browser will cache your entered data
- **Remember to come back and confirm data before sale**
- Confirm application content is correct

**When sale time arrives:**
1. Press **"Apply"** button on time
2. With good luck, you can successfully obtain tickets!

**Important Reminder:**
- This screen times out after 10 minutes without operation
- Recommend pressing the button once within 10 minutes before sale to maintain connection

---

### Step 5. Complete Application 

**Seeing the confirmation screen means you successfully obtained tickets! Congratulations!**
