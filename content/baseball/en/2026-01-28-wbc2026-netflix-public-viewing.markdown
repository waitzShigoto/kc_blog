## 2026 WBC Netflix Exclusive Streaming

The 2026 World Baseball Classic (WBC) will be exclusively streamed by Netflix in Japan. There will be no traditional TV broadcast.

### Streaming Overview

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| **Platform** | Netflix |
| **Format** | Live + On-demand |
| **Coverage** | All 47 games |
| **TV Broadcast** | None |
| **Other Streaming** | None (No Amazon Prime Video, DAZN, etc.) |

---

## Hometown Hero Public Viewing

Netflix will host the "2026 World Baseball Classic Hometown Hero Public Viewing" event.

### Event Overview

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| **Venues** | 30 locations across Japan |
| **Admission** | Free |
| **Host** | Players' hometown municipalities/schools |
| **Co-host** | Netflix |

### Eligible Games

- Tokyo Pool: All 4 Japan national team games
- Quarterfinals (if Japan advances)
- Semifinals (if Japan advances)
- Finals (if Japan advances)

### Hosting Requirements

1. Must be a Samurai Japan player's hometown or affiliated region
2. Local municipality or alma mater must serve as the host
3. Must sign a co-hosting agreement with Netflix
4. No admission fees or ticket sales allowed (free events only)

### Venue List

Detailed locations will be officially announced once confirmed. As the events will be held in each of the 30 players' hometowns, potential locations include:

- Shohei Ohtani: Oshu City, Iwate Prefecture
- Yoshinobu Yamamoto: Bizen City, Okayama Prefecture
- Munetaka Murakami: Kumamoto City, Kumamoto Prefecture
- Teruaki Sato: Nishinomiya City, Hyogo Prefecture
- Other players' hometowns

## Public Viewing at Commercial Venues

### Current Policy

Netflix has stated they will "carefully evaluate" public viewing at commercial venues (sports bars, restaurants, etc.).

### Important Notes

- Unauthorized screening at commercial venues may constitute copyright infringement
- Commercial facilities require separate licensing
- Detailed conditions to be announced later
