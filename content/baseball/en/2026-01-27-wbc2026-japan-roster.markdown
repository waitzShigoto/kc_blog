## Key Players

### Shohei Ohtani (Pitcher/DH)

2023 WBC MVP. Led the Dodgers to a World Series championship in 2024. Expected to compete as a two-way player.

### Yoshinobu Yamamoto (Pitcher)

Dodgers ace right-hander. Won the NPB pitching triple crown during his time in Japan.

### Munetaka Murakami (Infielder)

Moved to MLB White Sox in 2025. Hit the walk-off single in the 2023 WBC championship game.

### Seiya Suzuki (Outfielder)

Power-hitting outfielder for the Cubs. Expected to be a key piece in Japan's lineup.
