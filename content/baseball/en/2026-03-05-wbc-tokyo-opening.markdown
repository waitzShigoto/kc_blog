The **2026 World Baseball Classic (WBC)** Tokyo Pool C officially commenced today (March 5th) at the Tokyo Dome! The opening day featured two high-stakes matchups: Taiwan vs Australia and South Korea vs Czechia.

---

## Today's Match Results

### 12:00 Taiwan 0–3 Australia
Team Taiwan started their campaign with young ace **Lin Yu-min** on the mound. Lin delivered a solid performance, pitching 6 innings and allowing only 2 runs while racking up several strikeouts. However, the bats remained silent against Australia's Jack O'Loughlin and a lights-out bullpen led by Liam Hendriks. Australia secured the win 3-0, putting Taiwan in a difficult position early in the tournament.

### 19:00 South Korea 11–4 Czechia
The nighttime clash saw South Korea power past Team Czechia. Led by KBO superstar Kim Do-young, the South Korean offense exploded for 11 runs. They finished the game with a comfortable 11-4 win, signaling their intent to return to the global elite.

---

## Samurai Japan Status

The defending champion **Samurai Japan** had a rest day today. They held their final pre-tournament practice session at the Tokyo Dome this morning. Manager Hirokazu Ibata confirmed that **Yoshinobu Yamamoto** will take the mound for their opener tomorrow (3/6) at 19:00 against Team Taiwan.

Tomorrow's evening game is expected to be a sell-out as baseball fans anticipate the much-heralded clash between Japan and Taiwan.
