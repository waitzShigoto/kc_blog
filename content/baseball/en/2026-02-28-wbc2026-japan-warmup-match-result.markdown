As the countdown to the World Baseball Classic (WBC) 2026 continues, the favorite **Samurai Japan** is reaching its peak form!

In the second match of the "Luxas Samurai Japan Series 2026 Nagoya" held today (Feb 28) at the Vantelin Dome Nagoya, Samurai Japan showcased terrifying long-ball power, ultimately defeating the Chunichi Dragons **7-3** to sweep the two-game series.

---

## Match Highlights

### 1. Power Duo: Homers by Maki and Morishita
Early in the game, Japan's lineup put immense pressure on the Dragons' pitching staff:
*   **Shugo Maki**: Ignited the scoreboard with a towering solo home run to left field, giving Japan an early lead.
*   **Shota Morishita**: Followed up with another home run, showcasing his exceptional hitting ability and helping Japan establish early dominance.

### 2. The Big 5th Inning
The Japanese team launched a fierce offensive in the top of the 5th inning, scoring **5 runs** in a single frame to break the game wide open.

### 3. Solid Pitching Performance
Although the Dragons attempted a late comeback by scoring 3 runs, Samurai Japan's bullpen remained composed, effectively suppressing the opposition to secure the win.
