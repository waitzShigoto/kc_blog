The wait is finally over! The official WBC "Resale" platform has released new details.

You might have heard of resale options on Ticket Pia or e+ before, but this time, the service is specifically for **"Paper Tickets"** (Physical Tickets).

If you have extra tickets to sell or are still looking for one, read this guide carefully.

Official Announcement: [2026 WBC Resale (Tixplus)](https://tixplus.jp/feature/wbc2026/index.html)

---

## Key Highlights

1.  **Platform**: Operated by **Tixplus** (Chikepura).
2.  **Start Date**: **February 2, 2026 (Mon)** at 12:00 PM (JST Scheduled).
3.  **Price**: **Face Value Only**. No markups, no bargaining. Strict trading at the original ticket price plus fees.
4.  **Who is this for?**:
    *   **Sellers**: People holding **"Paper/Physical Tickets"**.
    *   **Buyers**: People wanting to buy tickets (will receive **"Electronic Tickets"**).
5.  **Required Account**: Both parties need a **Plus Member ID** (Free).
6.  **Required App**:
    *   **"Chikepura Electronic Ticket" App** (チケプラ電子チケット).
    *   Buyers must use the app for entry.

---

## For Sellers: Selling Your Paper Tickets

If your plans changed or you accidentally won duplicate paper tickets, this is the safe and legal way to let them go.

### Selling Process
1.  **Register Ticket**:
    *   Log in to Tixplus with your Plus Member ID.
    *   Upload a photo of your paper ticket (proof of possession).
    *   Enter seat details.
2.  **Wait for Match**:
    *   You will be notified once a buyer purchases your ticket.
3.  **Mail the Ticket**:
    *   **CRITICAL STEP!** Once sold, you MUST mail the **physical ticket** to the Tixplus office.
    *   Funds are released only after the office verifies your ticket.
4.  **Receive Payment**:
    *   Ticket price minus fees will be transferred to your account.

**Note**: Since mailing is required, please keep your ticket safe. Do not lose or damage it, or it may be rejected.

---

## For Buyers: Buying Official Resale Tickets

Lost all lotteries? Missed the general sale? This is your last legitimate chance! Since it's face value, you don't have to worry about scalper prices.

### Buying Process
1.  **Register Member**:
    *   Sign up for a [Plus Member ID](https://tixplus.jp/login/signup.php).
2.  **Download App**:
    *   Download the **"Chikepura Electronic Ticket"** (Tixplus) App.
    *   Log in and complete SMS verification (Usually requires a Japanese mobile number—overseas fans beware!).
3.  **Browse & Buy**:
    *   Check for available tickets on the Tixplus WBC 2026 page.
    *   Select your preferred date/seat and pay immediately.
4.  **Receive Ticket**:
    *   Upon purchase, the ticket is delivered to your **App**.
    *   Show the QR code/screen on your phone to enter the stadium.

## FAQ

### Q1: How is this different from Ticket Pia or Lawson Ticket Resale?
**A:** Previous platforms mostly handled "Electronic Tickets" bought on their own sites. Tixplus is specifically handling **"Printed Paper Tickets"**. If you picked up your ticket at a convenience store, this is your only official resale channel.

### Q2: Is it expensive? What are the fees?
**A:** According to the latest official fee structure, it costs a bit more than the original price to ensure safety and authenticity.

**【Fees for Sellers】**
*   **Listing Fee**: **10%** of ticket price per ticket.
    *   *Minimum fee: 550 JPY.*
*   **Transfer Fee**: **330 JPY** per transaction.
*   **Mailing Fee**: **600 JPY** (Letter Pack Plus) to mail the ticket to the office.

**【Fees for Buyers】**
*   **System Usage Fee**: **10%** of ticket price per ticket.
    *   *Minimum fee: 550 JPY.*
*   **Ticketing Fee**: **165 JPY** per ticket.

### Q3: Can overseas fans use this?
**A:** Great news! It has been confirmed that **a Japanese mobile number is NOT required** for overseas fans to use the Tixplus App. You should be able to register and participate in the resale without needing SMS verification from a Japanese carrier.
