The 2026 World Baseball Classic (WBC) Tokyo Pool C stage reaches its final chapter today (March 10th). At the sold-out Tokyo Dome, the host nation **Samurai Japan** will take on a spirited **Team Czechia** in a matchup that has captured the hearts of Japanese fans.

## Prime Time Matchup: Japan vs Czechia (19:00)

Samurai Japan currently sits comfortably atop Pool C with a 3-0 record. A victory tonight would mark their third consecutive undefeated pool stage in WBC history.

1. **Young Ace: Hiroya Miyagi**: Managing Director Hirokazu Ibata has called upon 24-year-old Orix Buffaloes star **Hiroya Miyagi** to start today's game. Known for his pinpoint control and deceptive changeup, Miyagi is tasked with shutting down the Czech hitters.
2. **Czechia's Resilient Spirit**: Despite their record, Team Czechia's sportsmanship and passion have earned them massive respect in Japan. They aim to play their best baseball tonight against a Japanese roster filled with MLB superstars.
3. **Pregame Atmosphere**: The vibe around Tokyo Dome is electric as fans Celebrate Japan’s advancement to the quarterfinals. New ticket information for the upcoming knockout rounds in Miami has also been released by the organizers.

## Pool C Quarterfinals Race

While Japan has secured the top seed, the race for the second ticket to Miami is a chaotic three-way tie between Team Taiwan, South Korea, and Australia, all sitting at 2-2. The final decision will be determined by Team Quality Balance (TQB) after the tonight's game.
