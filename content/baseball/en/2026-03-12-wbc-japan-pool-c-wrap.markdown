The Tokyo round of the 2026 World Baseball Classic (WBC) has come to a spectacular close. Amidst the cheers of a sold-out Tokyo Dome, **Samurai Japan** demonstrated their absolute dominance, finishing the preliminary stage with four consecutive wins and securing the top ticket to the championship rounds in Miami.

---

## Final Group Match Recount: Japan vs. Czechia

On the evening of March 10th at the Tokyo Dome, Team Japan faced their final opponent of the pool stage, the Czech Republic.

1. **Hiroya Miyagi's Mastery**: Orix Buffaloes left-hander **Hiroya Miyagi** started for Japan, pitching solid innings and dominating with his precise control and lethal changeup that kept the Czech hitters off balance throughout his outing.
2. **Explosive Offense**: The Japanese lineup broke the game open when Shohei Ohtani fueled the rally, while Munetaka Murakami connected for his first extra-base hit of the tournament. Japan ultimately secured a decisive victory against Czechia.
3. **Sportsmanship on Display**: After the final out, players from both teams exchanged hats and congratulations on the field. The Japanese fans gave the Czech team a standing ovation, celebrating the spirit of the game.
