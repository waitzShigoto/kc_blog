The 2026 World Baseball Classic (WBC) Tokyo Pool C stage is nearing its conclusion. While the host team **Samurai Japan** enjoyed a rest day today (March 9th), the nighttime battle between **South Korea and Australia** determined much of the remaining group dynamics.

---

## Today's Match Result: South Korea 7–2 Australia
Facing a resilient Australia squad, South Korea sent Lotte Giants ace **Park Se-woong** to the mound. The South Korean offense was clinical from the start, managing 10 hits and capitalizing on scoring opportunities against Australian starter Daniel McGrath. They eventually secured a 7-2 win, keeping themselves in the hunt for the second seed. Australia finishes their pool stage with a 2-2 record.

---

## Samurai Japan: Final Group Match Preview

After a day of recovery and bullpen sessions, **Samurai Japan** is set for their final preliminary match against **Team Czechia** tomorrow (March 10th) at 19:00.

1. **Starting Pitcher**: Japan has confirmed that Orix Buffaloes star left-hander **Hiroya Miyagi** will start the game. Known for his deceptive delivery and pinpoint control, Miyagi looks to stifle the passionate Czech hitters.
2. **Perfect Record Pursuit**: With a 3-0 record, Samurai Japan aims to finish the group stage undefeated to secure the top seed for the quarterfinals heading into Miami.
3. **Atmosphere**: Fans at the Tokyo Dome are already gearing up for tomorrow’s sell-out. While the Czech team is often considered an underdog, their performance throughout this WBC has earned huge respect from the Japanese public.

---

## Pool C Status Update
The current standings show Japan (3-0), while Australia, Taiwan, and South Korea are all tied at 2-2. The final determination for the runner-up in Pool C will come down to tie-breaker rules (TQB). Every run and out in tomorrow's remaining fixtures will be critical.
