## 2. Key Points for Lawson Ticket Purchasing Strategy

### 1. One Account Can Only Purchase 1 Japan Match

Same as the advance sale, for Japan matches, one account can only purchase tickets for 1 match.

To purchase multiple Japan matches, you need to prepare:
- Different accounts
- Different credit cards

**Based on past experience, accounts that already purchased tickets during advance sale may not be able to purchase again during general sale. It's recommended to use accounts that haven't obtained any tickets yet (please judge the actual situation yourself).**

If you violate the account purchase restrictions, it may delay your time, and basically you'll lose the opportunity.

### 2. Handling Fees Have Become Reasonable

The general sale handling fee is **935 yen/ticket**, which is much more reasonable compared to the advance sale's 3,300 yen/ticket.

### 3. Credit Card Required, Convenience Store Pickup

- Credit card required for purchasing
- Please confirm credit card information in advance (expiration date, security code, 3D verification reception method)
- Tickets are picked up at convenience stores, not mailed

- The system conducts a lottery among people in the waiting room and assigns you a number

**Key Strategy: Prepare as many devices as possible to get more waiting room lottery chances!**
