## What is Resale?

### Basic Concept

**Resale** means "secondary sale" or "ticket transfer":

| Japanese | Katakana | English | Chinese |
|------|--------|------|------|
| 再販売 | リセール | Resale | 転売 |

### Why Do We Need an Official Resale System?

#### Legitimate Needs
- Unexpected circumstances prevent attendance
- Duplicate purchases requiring refunds
- Friends unable to attend requiring ticket transfer

#### Fan Protection
- Prevent scalper price gouging
- Prevent fraud
- Ensure ticket authenticity
- Maintain fair trading
