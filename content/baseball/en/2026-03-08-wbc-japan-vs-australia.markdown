March 8th was a spectacular day for baseball fans at the Tokyo Dome. Both scheduled matches ended in 1-run differences, keeping the audience on the edge of their seats until the final out.

---

## Today's Match Results

### 12:00 Taiwan 5–4 South Korea (10 Innings)
One of the most fierce rivalries in international baseball lived up to the hype. Team Taiwan entered the 9th inning trailing but managed to tie the game, sending it to the "Tie-breaker" extra innings. In the top of the 10th, Taiwan's offense capitalized on a key sacrifice fly and an RBI single to take the lead. They held off South Korea in the bottom half to finish 5-4. Taiwan completes their pool play stage with a 2-2 record.

### 19:00 Australia 3–4 Japan
Samurai Japan faced a resilient Australian squad in the evening. MLB starter **Yusei Kikuchi** delivered a solid performance over 6 innings, but Australia's power hitters kept it close throughout. Japan eventually scored the go-ahead run in the late innings and closed the door for a 4-3 victory. Japan moves to 3-0 in Pool C, virtually guaranteeing them a spot in Miami.

## Looking Ahead

Samurai Japan has a rest day tomorrow (3/9). The evening slot will feature a high-stakes duel between South Korea and Australia. Japan's final group match is scheduled for March 10th against Czechia, where **Hiroya Miyagi** is expected to start.
