## 2026 WBC World Baseball Classic Overview

The 2026 World Baseball Classic is the sixth edition of the tournament, featuring 20 teams from around the world.

### Key Dates

| Stage | Dates |
|-------|-------|

| **Quarterfinals** | March 13 - March 14, 2026 |
| **Semifinals** | March 15 - March 16, 2026 |
| **Championship** | March 17, 2026 |

## Tournament Format

### Pool Play

- Each pool consists of 5 teams playing round-robin
- Top 2 teams from each pool advance to quarterfinals
- Total of 8 teams advance to knockout stage

### Quarterfinals

| Dates | Venue | Matchups |
|-------|-------|----------|
| March 13-14 | Houston Minute Maid Park | Pool A vs Pool B qualifiers |
| March 13-14 | Miami loanDepot Park | Pool C vs Pool D qualifiers |

If Team USA advances to quarterfinals, they will play in Houston

### Semifinals

| Dates | Venue |
|-------|-------|
| March 15-16 | Miami loanDepot Park |

### Championship

| Date | Venue |
|------|-------|

## Venue Information

### Minute Maid Park (Pool B + Quarterfinals)
- Location: Houston, Texas, USA
- Home Team: Houston Astros
- Feature: First time hosting WBC

### loanDepot Park (Pool D + Semifinals + Championship)
- Location: Miami, Florida, USA
- Home Team: Miami Marlins

### Tokyo Dome (Pool C)
- Location: Tokyo, Japan
- Capacity: Approximately 46,000
- Feature: Has hosted pool play in all five previous WBC editions

### Hiram Bithorn Stadium (Pool A)
- Location: San Juan, Puerto Rico
