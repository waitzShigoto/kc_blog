## Core Features

Designed for both visual engagement and data accuracy, the simulator includes several key features:

![WBC Tournament Simulator Initial Interface](/images/posts/2026-03-04-wbc-tournament-simulator/shot1_initial.png)

### 1. Multiple Simulation Modes
- **Simulate 1x**: Perfect for observing the randomness and surprises of a single tournament run.
- **Continuous Simulation (10x/100x)**: Uses mass data processing to eliminate random outliers and provide results closer to real performance levels. The process features smooth real-time animations, allowing you to watch every victory unfold.

### 2. Precise Data Statistics
The sidebar provides real-time rankings and stats, including:
- **Win Probability Rank**: Who is most likely to claim the ultimate championship trophy?
- **Top 8 Probability**: Which teams have the best chance to break through the Pool Stage and reach the quarterfinals?

![WBC Tournament Simulation Results and Statistics](/images/posts/2026-03-04-wbc-tournament-simulator/shot2_results.png)

### 3. Visualized Bracket Tree
Once a simulation completes, the system generates a full **Knockout Stage Bracket Tree**, showing every step from the Quarterfinals to the Finals.

### 4. Pool Stage Details
In addition to the knockout stages, the simulator displays detailed win-loss records for each group in the **Pool Stage**, giving you a comprehensive look at how teams performed in the preliminaries.

## Technical Highlights

- **Multi-language Support**: Full support for Chinese, English, and Japanese interfaces to serve baseball fans worldwide.

## How to Start Simulating?

1. Click the **"Simulate"** button at the top to run a single tournament.
2. For more accurate statistical results, try **"Simulate 100x"**.
3. To clear the history and start fresh, click the **"Reset Data"** button above the statistics section.
