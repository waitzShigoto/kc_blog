Great news for AI developers and power users! On April 4, Anthropic announced that to celebrate the launch of their flexible new billing tier—**"Usage Bundles"**—they will be distributing a one-time "free usage credit" to subscribers on active premium plans, with rewards going up to $200!

If you heavily rely on Claude, Claude Code, or Cowork, this extra balance is an absolute must-have. Follow the timeline and guide in this article to effortlessly claim your credits before they expire!

---

## What are "Usage Bundles"?

Traditionally, AI subscriptions fell into two distinct categories: standard individual monthly subscriptions and complex developer API accounts. Anthropic's new **Usage Bundles** bridge this gap perfectly. Designed for advanced users with heavier workloads, it allows subscribers to "purchase extra usage on-demand" without being forced to navigate or upgrade to developer API pricing. 

To celebrate the roll-out of this highly requested tier, Anthropic is generously rewarding their loyal subscribers with free credits to test it out.

---

## Free Credit Tier Breakdown

Anthropic is offering different credit amounts based on your current subscription plan. There are no sneaky fees attached:

*   **Claude Pro Plan:** Claim `$20`
*   **Claude Max 5x Plan:** Claim `$100`
*   **Claude Max 20x Plan:** Claim `$200`
*   **Claude Team Plan:** Claim `$200`

*Note: Once claimed, the credits are added directly to your account balance. They are valid for 90 days, and any unused portion will not roll over to the next period.*

---

## Eligibility & Deadlines

To be among the lucky users to claim this "limited-time giveaway," you must meet the following criteria:
1. **Subscription Status:** You must have been an active subscriber of the Pro, Max, or Team plan **before April 3, 2026, 9:00 AM (PT)**.
2. **Claim Deadline:** Make sure to claim your credits via the web interface **before April 17, 2026**. After this date, the offer will officially expire.

---

## 3 Easy Steps to Claim Your Credits

The claim process is straightforward, but please note that **it cannot currently be done via the Claude mobile app**. Ensure you are using the web version:

1. **Enable Extra Usage Permissions**
   * **Pro/Max Users:** Navigate to `Settings` → `Usage`.
   * **Team Users:** Navigate to `Organization Settings` → `Usage`.
   * Manually toggle the **`Extra usage`** feature to "ON".
2. **Click the Claim Button**
   Once enabled, return to your main interface or refresh the page. Look for the promotional banner at the top of the page and click the **`Claim`** button.
3. **Verify Your Balance**
   Once to claim is processed, the extra credits will instantly appear in your account. **You will not be charged**, and the credits are universally applicable across the Claude platform (including Claude Code and third-party integrations).

For users who rely heavily on AI for programming, data processing, and day-to-day writing, this is an incredible bonus. Log into your web account now and claim your rewards before the April 17 deadline!
